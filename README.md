# About Me

I'm a full-stack developer building products across web and mobile.

I design and ship end-to-end experiences — from polished interfaces to solid backends and production deployments. My work spans client apps, admin systems, APIs, and the operational layers that keep products running: authentication, payments, notifications, and ongoing maintenance.

I'm currently building **[Showcaxe](https://showcaxe.com)**, a managed website platform for business owners. It reflects how I like to work: clear product thinking, reliable engineering, and solutions that stay useful after launch, not just demos that look good once.

I care about maintainable architecture, thoughtful UX, and shipping software that teams and clients can depend on in the real world.
